<p>RX VPN is an Android client for V2Ray/Xray cores, built on the open-source
<a href="https://github.com/2dust/v2rayNG">v2rayNG</a> project. It does not ship or sell VPN service or
server configurations: you bring your own server, subscription link, or config file.</p>

<h3>What it does</h3>
<ul>
<li>Import configs by QR code, clipboard, file, or subscription link</li>
<li>Protocols supported by the bundled Xray core, including VLESS, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks,
SOCKS, WireGuard, TUIC, and Hysteria2</li>
<li>VPN mode with native tun2socks, plus proxy-only and root modes</li>
<li>App-based routing: all apps, selected apps only, or all apps except selected ones</li>
<li>Subscription groups, delay and real-ping tests, backup and restore, per-app settings</li>
</ul>

<h3>No ads, no tracking</h3>
<p>RX VPN contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK. The app does
not send usage data to the developer. Its only network connections are the ones you configure, plus
the update check against this repository's own releases page, which you can ignore.</p>
<p>Android 7.0 (API 24) and newer is supported.</p>

<h3>Requirements</h3>
<p>A working server configuration is required. Connection quality and availability depend on your
server, your operator, and your device network, so no universal connectivity guarantee is made.</p>
